# AI-Powered UI/UX Redesign for Mudah.my

> CSC649 Assignment 1 proposal: An Agentic AI and Multimodal Computer Vision framework to automate listing creation and buyer inquiry management in the Mudah.my marketplace context.

## Status

Draft - repository is private while the assignment is being prepared.

## Problem

Sellers on Mudah.my face high friction when manually uploading and tagging item listings, leading to incomplete ad descriptions and pricing inaccuracies. Simultaneously, buyers struggle with low-quality photos and delayed responses from sellers, increasing lead abandonment.

## Specialised Technology

Agentic AI and Multimodal Computer Vision

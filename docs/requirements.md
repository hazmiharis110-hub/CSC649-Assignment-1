# Part B: Functional and Non-Functional Requirements

## Functional Requirements
- **FR1:** The system shall auto-fill listing titles, categories, and condition tags within 3 seconds when images are uploaded.
- **FR2:** The system shall display a recommended listing price range calculated from historical market trends.
- **FR3:** The system shall provide an autonomous Agentic AI assistant to answer buyer questions when the seller is offline.
- **FR4:** The system shall allow buyers to filter search results by AI-computed Condition Scores.
- **FR5:** The system shall evaluate uploaded image resolution and defect visibility before listing publication.

## Non-Functional Requirements
- **NFR1 (Performance):** The visual auto-tagging process shall complete in under 2.0 seconds for up to 5 image uploads.
- **NFR2 (Usability):** The redesigned listing workflow shall require 4 clicks or fewer to publish an ad.
- **NFR3 (Security):** Automated AI chat messages shall display an explicit "AI Assistant" indicator badge.

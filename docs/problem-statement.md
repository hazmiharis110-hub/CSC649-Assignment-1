# Part A: Problem Statement, Users and Scope

## A1. Problem Statement

Sellers on Mudah.my must fill in many form fields by hand, so many ads have missing details or a wrong price. Buyers then ask the same questions again and again, and they wait for the seller to reply. This slows down sales, and some buyers leave before they buy anything. It matters because Mudah.my is Malaysia's largest online classifieds site and connects millions of Malaysians every month (Mudah.my, 2026).

### Evidence

- **Source:** Mudah.my (2026) describes itself as Malaysia's largest online classifieds marketplace, where millions of people buy and sell vehicles, property, electronics and home items every month. A busy site like this makes slow listing and slow replies a big problem.
- **Method I would use to measure the problem:** (1) count the form fields a seller must fill in to post an ad, and (2) check a sample of ads for the same item and count how many have no description or no condition. I did not measure this in this assignment.

## A2. Users and Stakeholders

| Group | Who they are | What they need |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Sellers (main users) | People and small shops who post ads | A fast way to post a full ad with a fair price |
| Buyers (main users) | People who search and contact sellers | Clear item details and quick answers |
| Platform moderators | Mudah.my staff who check ad quality and safety | Fewer poor ads and clear signs when a message is from AI |
| Mudah.my business | The company that runs the site | More complete ads and more sales |

## Scope

### In scope

- Suggest an ad title, category and condition label from the seller's photos.
- Show a price range for the item.
- Warn the seller when a photo is too small or unclear.
- Let an AI agent answer simple buyer questions when the seller is offline.
- Let buyers filter ads by condition label.

### Out of scope

- Changes to the main Mudah.my database.
- Payment, escrow or refund features.
- Delivery and parcel tracking.
- Building the real system. This assignment only explains and plans.

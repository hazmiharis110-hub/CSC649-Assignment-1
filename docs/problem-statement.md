# Part A: Problem Statement and Scope

## Problem Statement
Buyers and sellers on Malaysia's largest classifieds platform, Mudah.my, experience severe transaction friction due to unstructured listings, manual ad creation steps, and slow seller response times. Sellers find multi-step forms tedious, resulting in incomplete descriptions and drop-offs during ad creation. Buyers encounter low-resolution images, missing item specifications, and unverified seller disclosures.

## Users and Stakeholders
- **Pre-owned Buyers:** Seek accurate condition verification, dynamic pricing transparency, and fast responses.
- **Casual & Commercial Sellers:** Need rapid, automated listing tools with low operational overhead.
- **Platform Moderators:** Responsible for trust, safety, and listing quality control.

## Project Scope
- **In-Scope:** Automated listing auto-fill using Multimodal AI, dynamic pricing recommendations, autonomous Agentic AI response assistant, and condition filtering UI.
- **Out-of-Scope:** Core database refactoring, payment escrow integration, and third-party logistics tracking.
